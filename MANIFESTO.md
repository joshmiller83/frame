# Frame Manifesto

**Version:** Frame v0.0.1
**Methodology:** Semantic Frame Pattern (SFP)

## Why Frame exists

We are drowning in content but starving for context. Traditional keyword systems are too shallow, while formal ontologies are too brittle and complex for everyday use. As we rely more on probabilistic systems (LLMs), the need for a stable, human-readable signal of intent has become critical.

Frame exists to solve this by providing a minimal, standard way to declare the *nature* of a resource.

## Design Principles

1.  **Clarity beats precision.** It is better to be broadly understood than precisely ignored.
2.  **Whole words beat abbreviations.** We do not use cryptic codes. We use plain language (e.g., `Domain.Security`, not `D.SEC`).
3.  **At-a-glance understanding.** A Frame must be understood in seconds by a human.
4.  **Stability over cleverness.** The vocabulary should change rarely. Reliability is more valuable than novelty.

## Frame and AI

Frame is not "AI hype." It is a structural pattern for better data hygiene.

Frame does not tell models what to think.
Frame tells models how to look, not what to conclude.

By setting the interpretive posture—the "mode" of thinking—Frame reduces hallucination and improves alignment between human intent and machine processing.

## Conclusion

Frame is intentionally small. It is a humble layer of metadata designed to last.
